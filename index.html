Who I Am

About me

For over 5 years, I've been an artist first — translating that into beautiful, highly creative front-end work. I move between striking minimalism, magazine-style layouts, and esoteric UIs with celestial imagery, dramatic contrast, and Y2K chrome.

I don't just build websites; I design immersive experiences, usually with a few clever easter eggs hidden for the curious. Glassmorphic surfaces, parallax scrolling, on-hover effects like dissolving type or elements that "explode" on scroll — sometimes a cinematic video or a bit of sound to match the mood.

From AI-powered interfaces at NIELIT, to creative sites at SKIE and PW, to a UK-based hybrid internship at Apexure — my focus stays on beautiful UI/UX, smooth performance, and fully responsive pages, with a personal touch AI can't replicate.
